About

Assistant Professor, South Dakota State University, Ness School of Management and Economics

My fields are International Trade, Macroeconomics, and Spatial Economics.

My research studies spatial economic structures and how policies reshape where firms locate, how they organize, and regional development outcomes. I use production networks, trade theory, and economic geography to examine tax, monetary, environmental, and development policy.
